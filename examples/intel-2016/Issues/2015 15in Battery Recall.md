# Mid-2015 15" MacBook Pro Battery Overheating / Fire Recall

A federal fire-hazard recall over defective battery cells. Just before the 2016-2020 scope, but the canonical Intel-era battery hazard and frequently confused with in-scope 15" units, so it is carried as context.

## Symptoms

- Battery can overheat and pose a fire hazard.
- CPSC recorded 26 overheating reports including 5 minor burns and 1 smoke inhalation. About 432,000 US units were recalled.

## Suspected cause

Defective battery cells prone to overheating. Apple voluntarily recalled and replaced affected batteries free of charge. Affected units were also flagged to refuse charging via a macOS update and were banned from flights by the FAA (Aug 2019) until serviced.

## Affected scope

MacBook Pro (Retina, 15-inch, Mid-2015), sold primarily September 2015 to February 2017. This is a PRE-T2, PRE-butterfly model and falls just before the tool's 2016-2020 Intel scope. Carry it as historical context and disambiguation, not as an in-scope defect for T2/butterfly/2019-thermal units. A buyer cross-shopping older 15" stock should be warned.

## How to detect on-site

This is serial-number-based, so it maps to **manual Phase 1 serial-check context**. Check the serial against Apple's recall page, and for any 15" of this vintage verify whether the recall battery was replaced. **Phase 2 battery health** surfaces a degraded or failing battery (condition, max capacity, cycle count) but does NOT identify recall eligibility, which is serial-based.
